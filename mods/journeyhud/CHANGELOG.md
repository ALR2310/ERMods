# JourneyHUD - Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- Added damage numbers for burns and other effect damage on enemies.
- Added a square minimap shape.
- Improved the default colors of the damage numbers.
- Fixed poison, scarlet rot and frostbite timers missing with Elden Ring Reforged.
- Fixed the HUD edits not applying when SpiritMultiplier is installed too.
- Fixed the boss health bar sitting too low with Elden Ring Reforged.
- Fixed your buildup gauge missing with Elden Ring Reforged.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-11-18

- Added floating damage numbers above enemies.
- Added icons for the damage types and statuses an enemy is weak to.
- Added an option to choose how effect timers show when effects share an icon.
- Fixed the target size setting resizing what hangs from a boss's health bar.

## [1.5.2] - 2026-11-15

- Fixed the game crashing with ERSS 4, 5 and 6, QuestPath and the Steam overlay.
- Improved compatibility with other overlay mods.

## [1.5.1] - 2026-11-12

- Fixed the option to hide enemy health bars not working.
- Fixed the HUD options not working on Mod Engine 3 with mods that replace the HUD file.
- Fixed the map icons missing with mods that replace the menu sprites.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-11-11

- Added compatibility with the Ultrawide UI Fixes mod.
- Added dragging your own buildup gauge in the drag mode.
- Added an option to hide the health bars and damage numbers above enemies.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-08

- Added an option that puts a boss's posture and effects under the game's boss health bar.
- Added an option to move and resize the game's rune counter.
- Fixed dragging the minimap and the target panel being limited to part of the screen.
- The minimap no longer shows Torrent.
- Fixed the overlay not working with ER Item Wheel and ER Gesture Wheel by Maldron9955.
- Made the minimap's map and icons load much faster and reorganized the config.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-11-06

- Added an option to hide the minimap in Roundtable Hold.
- Fixed the minimap showing during cutscenes.

## [1.2.0] - 2026-11-03

- Added a drag mode (F7 by default) to move the minimap and the target bar with the mouse.
- Added an option to mark the hostile enemies near you as red dots on the minimap (off by default).
- Fixed the minimap being blank at the Mistwood after defeating Radahn.
- Fixed the minimap being blank at Crumbling Farum Azula.
- Fixed the option to hide the minimap in caves also affecting areas that are not caves.

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-02

- Added support for negative values in the layout offset.
- Added compatibility with mods that override menu sprites, for example Blue Markers.
- Added compatibility with HDR output, for example ERSS-FG.
- Added an option to delay the start of the overlay, for when it conflicts with another overlay mod.
- Added a hotkey to hide and show the minimap and the target panel.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

- Initial release
